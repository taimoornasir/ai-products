The product

Meet the lead responder that never lets a lead go cold

Instant response captures the moment. The follow-up engine keeps it alive. The AI response engine turns replies into conversations — and a controlled launch makes sure it all works before it goes live.

01 · Instant response

From form fill to first touch

Detects the new lead, personalizes the first message and contacts the prospect on approved channels — with no one copying information by hand.

  • A Meta instant form — or another approved source — creates one clean row in your Google Sheet, the operating record for everything that follows.

  • Required contact fields and consent are checked, and duplicates are caught so one lead enters first touch once.

  • first_name, service_interest, campaign and location become the variables your approved templates read from.

  • Same campaign intent, separate templates — an email body is never copied verbatim into a text message.

  • Delivery results go back to the record and the next follow-up is scheduled by your drip rules.

02 · Follow-up engine

A sequence, not a timer

The sequence continues until the lead changes state. Before every scheduled send, the workflow reads the current lead status and decides whether that touch is still appropriate.

  • Scheduled → check status → send, or stop and pause. Never a blind drip.

  • Reply on either channel, booking or qualification, opt-out, invalid contact details, rep pause, or conversion.

  • Different lead sources and offers can run their own templates and timing.

  • Email and SMS can run on separate schedules; cold leads move into a longer nurture path.

  • A salesperson can pause the automation for any lead — every touch respects it.

03 · AI response engine

When the lead replies, it becomes a conversation

The AI reads the reply, classifies intent against approved company knowledge, answers what it's allowed to — and routes everything else to a person.

  • Matches the reply to the lead record and pulls recent conversation context first.

  • Question, objection, booking intent or opt-out — then decides the allowed next action.

  • FAQs, availability and process questions, simple qualification, next steps and booking — from your instruction package.

  • Complaints, disputes, refunds, legal/medical/financial advice, custom pricing, sensitive topics and low confidence go to a person.

  • Status, intent and next step are written back — and follow-ups pause the moment a conversation starts.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Pricing and contract questions are handled by our sales team — book a demo and we'll scope it with you.

What is Lead Responder?
An AI automation product from Colab that responds to inbound leads while intent is still high: a personalized email and SMS on first touch, a state-aware follow-up sequence, and AI reply handling with escalation to a person.
How fast is the first response?
It's designed for near-real-time response. Actual delivery depends on your lead source, the automation platform, your email provider, API and carrier latency — so we don't promise a fixed number of seconds.
Which lead sources does it work with?
It's built for Meta lead campaigns and adapts to other approved sources — website forms, landing pages, and quote or consultation requests.
Do we need a CRM?
No. A Google Sheet can serve as the operating record. HubSpot, Salesforce or another CRM can be connected when you want activity there.
What if the AI says something off-script?
The AI only answers from an approved instruction package — company facts, offer, FAQ, booking and qualification rules. Complaints, disputes, refunds, legal/medical/financial advice, custom pricing, sensitive topics, low-confidence replies and anything outside approved knowledge go to a person.
Can a salesperson take over a conversation?
Yes. A rep can pause the automation for any lead, and every scheduled touch checks that status before it sends. Escalated conversations arrive with context attached.
How are consent and opt-outs handled?
Only consented channels and purposes are used. STOP and unsubscribe are honored immediately and future automated sends are suppressed. Requirements vary by country, carrier and campaign type, so consent, registration, sender identity and disclosures are configured per market — confirm specific obligations with your legal team.
Is Make.com the product?
No — Make.com is the orchestration layer in the implementation stack. Lead Responder is the product: the workflow design, templates, state logic, AI rules, guardrails and logging.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on your lead sources, channels and scope. Our team can walk you through the right setup after you get in touch.
